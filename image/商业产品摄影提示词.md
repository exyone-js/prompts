# 商业产品摄影提示词

## 目标
生成高质量的产品展示图，适用于电商、广告等商业场景。

## 示例 Prompt

```
Generate a commercial product photo of a minimalist smartwatch.

## Requirements
- Product: Round dial, black silicone strap, single button on the right side
- Style: Apple-like minimalist aesthetic, clean white background
- Lighting: Soft studio lighting, subtle shadow on the ground
- Angle: 45-degree isometric view, dial slightly tilted toward camera
- Details: Screen shows a fitness tracking interface with heart rate data
- Mood: Premium, modern, technological
- Composition: Product centered, occupying about 60% of the frame
- Output: 4K resolution, photorealistic, suitable for e-commerce hero image
```

## 技巧
- 先描述主体，再描述风格和环境
- 指定光线、角度、构图等摄影参数
- 用参考品牌或风格关键词引导效果
- 中文用户建议用英文写 prompt，AI 对英文图像描述的理解通常更好
